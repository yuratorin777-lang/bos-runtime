## О директории INVESTORS

INVESTORS содержит все материалы, необходимые для привлечения инвестиций — от pitch презентаций до детальных финансовых моделей и due diligence документов.

---

## Структура INVESTORS

### Pitch Materials
Презентационные материалы:
- **Pitch Deck** — основная презентация для инвесторов
- **One Pager** — краткое описание на одной странице
- **Executive Summary** — развернутое executive summary
- **Demo Script** — сценарий демонстрации продукта

### Financial Models
Финансовые модели и прогнозы:
- **Financial Model** — 3-5 year financial projections
- **Unit Economics** — расчет юнит-экономики
- **Cap Table** — капитализационная таблица
- **Fundraising Plan** — план привлечения инвестиций

### Due Diligence
Материалы для due diligence:
- **Legal Documents** — юридические документы
- **IP & Patents** — интеллектуальная собственность
- **Team Bios** — биографии команды
- **Customer References** — референсы клиентов
- **Technical Architecture** — техническая документация

### Investor Communication
Коммуникация с инвесторами:
- **Investor Updates** — регулярные обновления
- **FAQs** — часто задаваемые вопросы
- **Meeting Notes** — заметки с встреч
- **Follow-up Materials** — материалы для follow-up

## Pitch Deck Structure

### Standard Pitch Deck (12-15 слайдов)

**1. Cover Slide**
- Company name & logo
- Tagline
- Contact info

**2. Problem**
- Clear problem statement
- Market pain points
- Current alternatives failing

**3. Solution**
- How BOS solves the problem
- Key differentiators
- Why now

**4. Product Demo**
- Screenshots/video
- Key features
- User experience

**5. Market Opportunity**
- TAM/SAM/SOM
- Market trends
- Growth drivers

**6. Business Model**
- Revenue streams
- Pricing strategy
- Unit economics

**7. Traction**
- Current metrics
- Key milestones
- Customer testimonials

**8. Competitive Landscape**
- Competitors overview
- Competitive advantages
- Positioning

**9. Go-to-Market Strategy**
- Customer acquisition
- Sales process
- Marketing channels

**10. Team**
- Founder(s) background
- Key hires
- Advisors

**11. Financial Projections**
- 3-5 year revenue forecast
- Key assumptions
- Break-even timeline

**12. The Ask**
- Amount raising
- Use of funds
- Milestones to achieve
- Expected timeline

**13. Appendix**
- Additional details
- Technical deep-dive
- Extra metrics

## Investor Meeting Preparation

### Before Meeting

**Research investor:**
- [ ] Review portfolio companies
- [ ] Understand investment thesis
- [ ] Find common connections
- [ ] Prepare personalized pitch

**Prepare materials:**
- [ ] Pitch deck ready
- [ ] One-pager printed/PDF
- [ ] Demo environment tested
- [ ] Follow-up materials ready

**Practice:**
- [ ] Rehearse pitch (10 min version)
- [ ] Prepare for tough questions
- [ ] Demo walkthrough smooth
- [ ] Know numbers cold

### During Meeting

**Opening (2 min):**
- Thank them for time
- Quick intro (who you are)
- Ask if they have time constraints

**Pitch (10 min):**
- Walk through deck
- Focus on problem → solution → why now
- Show demo if time allows

**Q&A (15-20 min):**
- Answer questions directly
- Be honest about unknowns
- Show data when available
- Don't BS

**Closing (3 min):**
- Summarize key points
- State the ask clearly
- Next steps
- Thank them

### After Meeting

**Within 24 hours:**
- [ ] Send thank you email
- [ ] Share promised materials
- [ ] Answer follow-up questions
- [ ] Update CRM/tracker

**Track:**
- [ ] Investor interest level
- [ ] Next steps agreed
- [ ] Timing for decision
- [ ] Additional info needed

## Investor FAQs

### Business Model

**Q: How do you monetize?**
A: SaaS subscription model. $499/mo (Pro) to $50K/mo (Enterprise).
Revenue from subscriptions + usage overages.

**Q: What's your CAC:LTV?**
A: Projected 15:1. CAC ~$8K, LTV ~$120K (5 year retention).

**Q: How do you compete with free AI tools?**
A: We're not competing with tools. We're the OS layer that
orchestrates ALL tools. Value is in orchestration, not access.

### Market

**Q: Who are your target customers?**
A: AI-first companies (Series A-C) and Enterprise (Fortune 5000).
Companies spending $10K+/month on AI with reliability needs.

**Q: How big is the market?**
A: $150B+ TAM in Business AI Operations. Growing 45% annually.
We're creating new category: AI Operating Systems.

**Q: What about competition?**
A: No direct competitors in "AI OS" category. Adjacent players
(aggregators, dev tools) solve different problems.

### Product & Technology

**Q: What's defensible?**
A: Architectural moat (self-healing orchestration), network
effects (more usage = better routing), category creation advantage.

**Q: Can't big tech build this?**
A: Could, but won't. Google/MS/Amazon want vendor lock-in.
We're vendor-agnostic by design. That's the key value.

**Q: How do you handle AI provider pricing changes?**
A: Multi-provider strategy. Always route to best price/performance.
Built-in cost optimization. Risk distributed.

### Traction & Fundraising

**Q: What's your current traction?**
A: Runtime MVP complete. Demo-ready. Currently raising pre-seed
to build production version and acquire first customers.

**Q: Why raise now?**
A: Perfect timing window. AI matured but fragmented. 12-18 months
to establish category leadership before big tech notices.

**Q: What milestones with this round?**
A: Build production product, hire core team (3-4 people),
acquire 10+ paying customers, achieve $10K MRR, validate PMF.
